Data Privacy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Operator: Rajnish Systems (rajnishsystems.in)

1. Privacy Commitment

At ChatBot, we believe user privacy is fundamental. This section outlines how we collect, store, and safeguard your personal details while you interact with our AI conversational services.

2. What Personal Data We Collect

  • Account Identifiers: Verified email address, username, and encrypted passwords. Passwords are never saved in plaintext; they are irreversibly hashed using BCrypt.
  • OAuth2 Single Sign-On: When signing in with Google, we receive only your email and public profile ID to authenticate your session.
  • User Conversations: Text queries, chat history, and uploaded files (PDFs, PowerPoint decks, Word docs, text files, and images) submitted for AI analysis.
  • Token & Usage Metrics: Daily API interaction counts and token usage to maintain rate limits (10 calls/day for guests, 50 calls/day for registered users).

3. How Your Data Is Protected

Your data is stored in a private, encrypted PostgreSQL database with vector embeddings managed by PGVector. We apply strict security principles:

  • Stateless JSON Web Tokens (JWT) for secure session authorization.
  • One-way salted cryptographic hashing (BCrypt) for passwords.
  • Encrypted TLS/HTTPS transport for all API traffic.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third parties.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have complete control over your data. At any time, you can visit Profile & Settings to update your credentials or click Delete Account.

Deleting your account immediately and irreversibly wipes your profile, session history, and all chat records from our database.

Usage Policy & Project Scope

Last Updated: October 2026 | Project Type: Learning & Portfolio Architecture

Educational & Portfolio Purpose:

ChatBot is designed, engineered, and maintained as a modern full-stack AI learning project by Rajnish Chauhan (Rajnish Systems). It serves as a practical implementation of Spring AI, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), vector databases, and real-time streaming architectures.

1. Acceptable Use Policy

When using this educational platform, users agree to:

  • Use the system respectfully for testing, experimentation, and research questions.
  • Refrain from attempting prompt injections, abusive language, or automated DDoS requests against the backend APIs.
  • Respect system quotas: Guest accounts are capped at 10 daily queries, and registered accounts are allocated 50 daily queries (resetting every 24 hours).

2. AI Output & Accuracy Disclaimer

ChatBot utilizes large language models and semantic document retrieval. While designed to provide high-quality, contextual answers:

  • AI responses are generated automatically and should be verified for factual or technical accuracy.
  • The assistant does not provide certified medical diagnoses, legally binding advice, or professional financial guidance.
  • The service is provided on an educational "as-is" basis without commercial service-level guarantees.
